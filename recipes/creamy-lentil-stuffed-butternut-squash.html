Creamy Lentil Stuffed Butternut Squash

  • Category: Vegetarian
  • Servings: 4
  • Prep time: 20 min
  • Cook time: 1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium butternut squashes
  • Olive or avocado oil, for roasting
  • Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • 2/3 tbsp olive oil (more if not using nonstick)
  • 1⅓ large yellow onions, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2/3 tbsp chopped fresh sage
  • 1⅓ tsp chopped fresh rosemary
  • 1⅓ tbsp tomato paste
  • 3.6 tbsp (53⅓ ml) dry red wine (pinot noir, shiraz/syrah, or malbec)
  • 2/3 cup (126⅔ g) green or brown lentils
  • 1.8 cups (426⅔ ml) vegetable broth
  • 2/3 bay leaf
  • 2/3 tsp sweet or hot paprika
  • 1⅔ tbsp tahini
  • 1⅓ tsp white miso paste
  • 1⅓ to 2 tsp good-quality balsamic vinegar
  • 2/3 cup roasted butternut squash (from the scooped-out halves)
  • 1⅓ tbsp tahini (for sauce)
  • 2/3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 to 4 tbsp water or vegetable broth, to thin sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Chopped Italian flat-leaf parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Halve squashes and scoop out seeds. Place cut side up on a baking sheet, rub with oil, and season with salt and pepper. Roast 45 to 50 minutes until fork tender and lightly browned.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Sauté onions with a pinch of salt for 8 to 9 minutes until mostly browned. Add garlic, sage, and rosemary; cook 2 minutes. Add tomato paste; cook 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Reduce heat to medium, add red wine, deglaze, and cook until the liquid evaporates and the alcohol smell dissipates, 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Add vegetable broth, lentils, bay leaf, and paprika. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a rapid simmer until the lentils are tender and most liquid is absorbed, about 30 minutes.
  5. Reduce heat to low; stir in tahini, miso, and balsamic vinegar. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. When the squash is cool enough to handle, scoop out the flesh, leaving a ½ to ¾ inch border.
  7. Transfer 1 cup squash flesh to a food processor (reserve the rest for another use).
  8. Reduce oven to 350°F.
  9. Stuff lentil filling into the squash cavities; bake 15 minutes until warmed through.
  10. Make the sauce: blend the 1 cup squash flesh with tahini, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Stream in water or broth until pourable but thick.
  11. Serve stuffed squash drizzled with tahini sauce, garnished with parsley.
