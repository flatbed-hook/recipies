Sheet Pan Chicken and Potatoes with Honey-Garlic Butter Sauce

A one-pan dinner featuring crispy potatoes and juicy chicken coated in a glossy honey-garlic butter sauce. Comes together quickly with minimal effort.

  • Category: Other
  • Servings: 4
  • Prep time: 10 min
  • Cook time: 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (1.5 lb)
  • 1 lb baby Yukon gold potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp oil (olive or avocado)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp Kinder's Buttery Steakhouse seasoning
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ½ cup honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss potatoes and chicken with oil and dry spices on a sheet pan in a single layer.
  2. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, flipping the potatoes at the 15-minute mark, until crispy.
  3. While baking, melt butter in a small saucepan. Add garlic, sizzle 30 seconds, then stir in honey, salt, and pepper. Simmer 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Drizzle sauce over finished chicken and potatoes. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Notes

Per serving: about 460 calories, 30 g protein, 36 g carbs, 22 g fat, 3 g fiber.
