Greek Lemon Chicken

Juicy, tender, and crisp chicken thighs baked coated in a delicious lemon-herb marinade.

  • Category: Other
  • Servings: 8 pieces
  • Prep time: 15 min
  • Cook time: 40 min

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare marinade: Mix oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, mustard, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Marinate: Place chicken in a large bowl, add marinade, toss, and refrigerate 1 to 2 hours (up to 8 hours).
  3. Transfer: Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Arrange chicken skin-side up in a baking dish, pour remaining marinade over top.
  4. Bake: Cook 40 to 45 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Broil 2 to 3 minutes for extra crispy skin if desired.

Notes

Do not marinate longer than 3 hours with acidic marinades; texture becomes mushy. Boneless thighs cook faster; check at 30 to 35 minutes. Freezes well up to 3 months; reheat gently with added moisture.
