Potato Kugel

  • Category: Vegetarian
  • Servings: About 2 pans
  • Prep time: Not specified
  • Cook time: 2 hours, or longer if needed

Ingredients

  • 9 variety-size gold potatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • ¾ cup olive oil or regular oil
  • 1 onion
  • About 1 tbsp salt

Instructions

  1. Grate potatoes and onion (do not strain the liquid; it all goes in).
  2. Mix grated potatoes and onion with eggs, oil, and salt.
  3. Spray two pans well; divide the mixture between them, keeping it fairly shallow for even baking.
  4. Bake at 350°F for 2 hours, or longer if needed.
