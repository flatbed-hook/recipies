Copycat Blueberry Mamma Chia Squeeze

A simple, healthy snack made from pureed blueberries, chia seeds, a touch of honey, and water.

  • Category: Other
  • Servings: 2
  • Prep time: 10 min
  • Cook time: None (2 hr resting/refrigeration)

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup frozen blueberries
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp honey (or maple syrup for infants under 1 year)

Instructions

  1. Blend blueberries, water, and honey on high until pureed, about 1 minute.
  2. Add chia seeds to a jar; pour the blueberry puree over the seeds.
  3. Stir in lemon juice; mix thoroughly for even seed distribution.
  4. Refrigerate 2 hours or overnight until thickened.
  5. Consume with a spoon or straw; store refrigerated up to one week.

Notes

Use fresh or frozen fruit; fresh requires less water. Experiment with strawberries, mixed berries, or fruit-vegetable combinations. Store in reusable pouches for convenience; freezer-safe in individual portions.
