Lemon-Garlic Chicken Pitas with Feta Tzatziki & Roasted Bell Peppers

A bright and flavorful meal featuring lemon-garlic chicken, charred roasted peppers, and creamy feta tzatziki, all stuffed in warm pitas.

  • Category: Other
  • Servings: 4 (1 pita per person)
  • Prep time: 15 min active, plus 30 min marinating
  • Cook time: 15 to 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 pita breads
  • 1 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 cucumber, grated
  • 2 roasted red bell peppers
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • Fresh dill or parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken in lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper for a minimum of 30 minutes.
  2. Cook chicken by grilling (6 to 8 minutes per side) or baking at 425°F for 18 to 22 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  3. Prepare tzatziki by combining Greek yogurt, crumbled feta, grated cucumber (excess moisture squeezed out), garlic, and herbs. Season to taste.
  4. Toast pita in a skillet for 30 to 60 seconds per side.
  5. Assemble pitas with sliced chicken, roasted peppers, and tzatziki. Serve immediately.

Notes

Marinate longer for deeper flavor. Use low-sodium options to reduce overall sodium content. Keep components separate if storing, to prevent sogginess.
