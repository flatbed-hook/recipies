Tabbouleh

  • Category: Salads
  • Servings: 4
  • Prep time: 40 min
  • Cook time: None (no cooking required)

Ingredients

  • 3.6 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • 0.2 tsp ground coriander
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • 2 cups finely chopped curly parsley (about 1½ bunches)
  • ½ cup diced English cucumber
  • ½ cup cored and diced tomato
  • 0.4 cup cooked bulgur wheat
  • 3.6 tbsp finely chopped fresh mint
  • 1½ scallions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, coriander, and cinnamon in a large bowl. Add parsley, cucumber, tomato, bulgur, mint, and scallions; toss to combine.
  2. Season to taste and chill until ready to serve.
