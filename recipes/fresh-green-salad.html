Fresh Green Salad

Fresh, healthy, and delicious. Leftovers are even better the next day.

  • Category: Salads
  • Servings: Not specified
  • Prep time: Not specified
  • Cook time: None (no cooking required)

Ingredients

  • ½ green cabbage, shredded
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced thin
  • 4 celery ribs, chopped (leaves included if available)
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 1 green apple, thinly sliced into strips
  • 2 Serrano chilies with seeds (optional, for a kick)
  • ½ bunch fresh mint, chopped
  • ½ bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Olive oil
  • Fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp salt

Instructions

  1. Chop, slice, toss, and enjoy.
