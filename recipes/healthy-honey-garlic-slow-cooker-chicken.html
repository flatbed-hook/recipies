Healthy Honey Garlic Slow Cooker Chicken

This healthy honey garlic chicken recipe takes no time to throw together using your crock-pot or slow cooker. Just five ingredients and kid-friendly!

  • Category: Other
  • Servings: Not specified
  • Prep time: Not specified
  • Cook time: 4 hours on high

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic

Instructions

  1. Spray crock-pot with non-stick spray.
  2. Mix honey, soy sauce, water, vinegar, and garlic in a bowl.
  3. Place chicken breasts in crock-pot.
  4. Pour the sauce over chicken.
  5. Cook on high for 4 hours.

Notes

Serve over rice with roasted vegetables. Leftover chicken works well on salads, sandwiches, and soups. One commenter recommended adding chicken thighs to prevent dryness.
